Privacy policy

Tattva · Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

Tattva helps you track what you eat. We collect only what the app needs to work, we don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics or tracking, and we don’t sell your data.

Your account

You sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID, and use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in.

Sign-in and accounts are run by Supabase, the service that hosts our database.

What you log

The app stores what you enter: your meal log, saved foods, goals and the details in “About you” (sex, age, height, weight, activity level, diet, weight goal and the water you log). These are used to work out your calorie and nutrient goals and to show your insights.

This data is kept on your phone and backed up to our Supabase database so you can sign in on another phone and get it back. Database rules allow only your account to read or change it.

Meal scans and Tavi estimates

When you use Meal scan, the photos you take are sent to our server and on to Google’s Gemini, the model behind Tavi, which identifies the food or reads the label. When you ask Tavi in Search, the words you typed are sent the same way. We don’t store the photos or descriptions on our server; a scanned photo stays on your phone with the item.

We use Gemini’s free service. Under Google’s terms for it, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Don’t photograph or type anything you wouldn’t want shared, such as documents, people or personal details.

To keep the service fair, we record the time of each scan or estimate against your account to apply a daily limit.

Recipes, swaps, ideas and reviews from Tavi

When you ask for a recipe, a healthier swap, meal ideas or a weekly review, we send what Tavi needs to Google’s Gemini: the name of the dish or food, and for swaps, ideas and the review also your goal, diet, the foods you’ve eaten lately and your week’s totals. We don’t send your name or email address. We keep the recipes, swaps, ideas and reviews we get back with your account, so opening them again doesn’t ask again.

Tavi’s take on Insights is worked out on our server from your totals; we use your account to know whether you have Pro or are still in your free week.

If you report something Tavi wrote, we store the report (what it was about and why) against your account so we can look at it.

Pro

Whether your account has Pro, and until when, is kept on our server. Paying for Pro goes through Google Play. Google Play and RevenueCat, the service that manages subscriptions for us, handle your purchase and are told your account ID; we never see or store your payment details.

Barcodes and search

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes and take the photos you choose to scan.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. Signing out removes it from the phone but keeps your backup, so it comes back when you sign in again.

To delete your account and all backed-up data, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. It’s deleted straight away and can’t be undone.

Children

Tattva is for people aged 15 and over; the app doesn’t accept a younger age, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we’ll update this policy and the date below, and point out important changes in the app.
