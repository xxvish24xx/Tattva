Delete your account

Tattva

In the app

Open Tattva, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Your account and everything backed up with it (profile, goals, meal log, saved foods and recipes, and Tavi’s saved suggestions) are deleted straight away. This can’t be undone.

If you can’t open the app

Reinstall Tattva, sign in with the same Google account, and follow the steps above. Signing in again brings your account back so you can delete it.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, straight away:

  • your sign-in details (name, email address and account ID from Google);
  • your profile and goals (age, sex, height, weight, activity level, diet and weight goal);
  • your meal log, saved foods and recipes, and water log;
  • what Tavi saved for you (recipes, swaps, meal ideas, Tavi’s take and weekly reviews);
  • your Pro status record and any reports you sent about Tavi’s content.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you, and there is no extra retention period. Photos you scanned are never stored on our servers. A copy kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall Tattva or sign out.
